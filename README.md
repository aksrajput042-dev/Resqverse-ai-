🚨 RESQVERSE AI

Intelligent Emergency Coordination & Unmet-Need Detection Platform

Tagline: See the Need. Track the Response. Close the Gap.

---

🌍 About the Project

RESQVERSE AI is an intelligent emergency coordination platform designed to help organise emergency requests, track unresolved needs and suggest suitable resource matches.

During disasters such as floods, storms, fires and major accidents, emergency information can become scattered across different communication channels.

RESQVERSE AI aims to bring this information into one centralised dashboard so coordinators can understand what help is needed and which requests are still unresolved.

Our main goal is to make unmet emergency needs visible.

---

❗ Problem Statement

During emergencies, several challenges can occur:

- Emergency requests arrive through different channels.
- Some requests remain unverified or unassigned.
- Duplicate reports create confusion.
- Available resources are difficult to coordinate.
- Language barriers affect communication.
- Unresolved requests may be overlooked.

RESQVERSE AI focuses on solving these coordination challenges.

---

💡 Our Solution

RESQVERSE AI provides a centralised digital workspace for emergency coordination.

It helps users:

- Submit emergency assistance requests.
- Organise requests into categories.
- Identify unresolved requests.
- Monitor request status.
- View available resources.
- Suggest resource matches.
- Track updates through a case timeline.

AI suggestions are reviewed by human coordinators before action.

---

✨ Key Features

1. AI Need Detector

Analyses request descriptions and suggests categories such as:

- Food
- Drinking Water
- Shelter
- Medical Assistance
- Transportation

2. Unmet-Need Detector

Highlights requests that are:

- Awaiting verification.
- Not yet assigned.
- Waiting for follow-up.
- Without recent status updates.

3. Smart Resource Matcher

Suggests possible matches between verified emergency requests and available resources.

4. Emergency Dashboard

Displays:

- Total Requests
- Pending Requests
- Verified Requests
- Assigned Requests
- Completed Requests

5. Language Support

Helps translate and summarise emergency request information.

6. Low-Connectivity Support

Allows local draft saving and supports offline access to selected information.

7. Case Timeline & Audit Trail

Tracks request updates, timestamps and coordinator actions.

---

🔄 How RESQVERSE AI Works

1. REQUEST – A person submits an assistance request.
2. STRUCTURE – AI suggests the request category.
3. VERIFY – A human coordinator verifies the information.
4. MATCH – The system suggests available resources.
5. FOLLOW-UP – The coordinator tracks the request until closure or escalation.

🌊 Example Use Case

Imagine a flood emergency.

Multiple people submit requests for drinking water, food and shelter.

RESQVERSE AI organises these requests and displays them on a central dashboard.

---

🎯 Our Innovation

The main focus of RESQVERSE AI is its Unmet-Need Detection System.

Instead of simply collecting emergency requests, our platform focuses on identifying which requests are still waiting for verification, assignment or follow-up.

This creates a clearer and more transparent emergency coordination workflow.

🔐 Safety & Responsibility

RESQVERSE AI is a coordination-support platform, not an emergency service.

- AI recommendations require human review.
- Resource assignments require human approval.
- Demo data should be fictional.
- Personal information should be protected.
- Offline drafts are not confirmed delivered requests.

For immediate danger, contact official emergency services.
